About Ruchita Nagar

Senior Software Engineer at Tietoevry, Bengaluru

I’m Ruchita Nagar, a full-stack engineer and team lead based in Bengaluru, India. I have spent 5+ years delivering enterprise web applications across telecom, AI, and energy with React, Python, and Django, and I currently work as a Senior Software Engineer at Tietoevry. Online I go by ruchita-nagar.

Where I started

I did my Bachelor of Science at Sant Singaji Institute of Science and Management, Sandalpur, from 2013 to 2016, and picked up early industry exposure through internships at Neev Information Technologies (2014), Singaji Software Solutions (2015), and Incture (2016).

In 2018 I secured All India Rank 86 in NIMCET, the national entrance exam for NIT MCA programs, which took me to the National Institute of Technology, Tiruchirappalli (NIT Trichy). I completed my Master of Computer Applications there in 2021 with a CGPA of 8.38.

During the MCA I won first place in the Thinklytics event at LOGIN’19, the national level MCA technical symposium at PSG College, Coimbatore, and interned at Trawello HealthyKID, where I built an Android app that lets school health workers save and access students’ medical health data, using MIT App Inventor and Google Firebase.

My career so far

I began my career at Halliburton in August 2021 as a Software Developer. I built reusable React components and REST API integrations for Assisted Horizon Interpretation (AHI) and Assisted Seismic Interpretation (ASI), which visualize seismic data for enterprise users, improved performance by cutting unnecessary re-renders, redundant API calls and slow Django queries, and wrote Jest unit tests.

From October 2023 to March 2026 I was a Software Developer at Dataworkz, working on an enterprise AI-powered platform. I built Agent Builder interfaces, agent configuration, tool recommendations and workflow management in React, integrated services such as PubMed, Slack, and Google Drive, and engineered Python, Django and DRF backends with authentication, RBAC, user management, and payment integration.

In April 2026 I joined Tietoevry as a Senior Software Engineer. I build features for the NetCloud Manager (NCM) platform that manages private 5G/LTE networks, covering RAN configuration, SIM management, authentication, and monitoring, and I lead a team of 4+ developers through code reviews, task planning, and technical guidance.

What I build

Most of my work is full stack: React interfaces on the front, Python and Django services and REST APIs behind them, PostgreSQL for data, and AWS or Azure for cloud. I care about secure, scalable, high-quality software, and I use AI tools like GitHub Copilot and Claude to ship faster.

Outside of work I have built:

  • DriveMaster, a web-based file browser with secure folder sharing between registered users and role-based access control.
  • LS’it (Let’s Sell It), a campus marketplace for buying and selling reusable items at NIT Trichy, with real-time listing updates.
